How this portal helps

For Veterans

Create a single, up-to-date record of your service, experience and preferences. NCC authorities can discover and shortlist you for relevant roles.

For NCC Units & HQs

Quickly locate suitable veterans by branch, rank, experience and location. Track applications and streamline approvals.

For Higher HQ

Gain a consolidated view of sanctioned strength, vacancies and veteran deployment across Directorates and Units.

Security & Data Handling

The system is designed to work on controlled infrastructure. Personal and service details are visible only to authorised NCC staff on a need-to-know basis.
